about me

i grew up immersed in forests and water. as a teenager i was a competitive diver and a national medallist. now i train young people to be their best, in the water and out of it.

everything i teach comes back to one idea: moving well in everyday life. water is where i begin, with keeping safe, rescuing others, playing and exploring underwater. from there we build strength, conditioning and a deeper sense of the body.

my practice

swim

from first float to confident freestyle. i correct strokes and train them one by one, build comfort in deep water, and open the door to open-water swimming and water survival. groups or one-on-one, for kids and every age after.

dive

overcome the fear of jumping, work through the mental blocks, and free your body through movement. i train divers from first jump to competition and personal bests, on springboard, platform, cliff diving, water sports and more. and i'm always up for an adventure.

move

lift, jump, pull, throw, hit, and take a leap. i help you understand how your body moves and how you eat, so you build fundamentals strong enough for anything. i teach strength and conditioning, somatic work, calisthenics and balance. beginners start with bodyweight basics; pro athletes train at high intensity. every age is welcome.
